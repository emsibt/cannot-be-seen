Does capture exclusion actually work?

This page captures your screen using the exact same browser API that Google Meet and Teams-on-web use, so what you see here is what your meeting participants would see.

Frozen snapshot

How to read this. Pick Entire Screen in the picker, then move a protected window over this page. In the live capture it should be completely absent — you'll see whatever is behind it — while remaining fully visible on your real screen. Press Win+Shift+S and it will be missing from the snip too.
  1. Open a protected window from the dashboard and put it over this page.
  2. Click Start screen capture and choose your whole screen.
  3. The protected window is visible to you, but not in the left-hand preview.
If it does show up in the preview, capture exclusion is not being honoured and the tool will not hide it from a real meeting either.
Note: sharing a single window instead of the whole screen works differently — pickers let you share the protected window directly, and anything you deliberately share is captured normally. Full-screen sharing is the case this protects against.
